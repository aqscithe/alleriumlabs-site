Allerium Labs is the name of my game studio. The purpose is to showcase what we're working on, the games we've released, interact with our community, and bring joy, scares, learning and entertainment to people through games.

## Design Philosophy

- Capture Attention
- Engage the Senses
- Add Value 
- Meaningful Engagement & Content
- Visual Clarity and Conciseness
- Responsive Design
- Be Cool, Be Fun, Be On The Bleeding Edge
- Show, Don't Tell
- Graphics, Physics, AI & Immersive Systems
- Make Pages Feel Alive — Give the User Toys

A page should feel alive, not static. Sprinkle in small interactive or animated
"toys" the user can either just watch or actively play with — they're the extra
touch that makes a page memorable. Lean toward smooth, continuous motion over
sharp/repetitive ticking: smooth movement reads as calm and lively, whereas
constant ticking can feel anxiety-inducing (e.g. the contact page's response-time
clock rests, then sweeps its minute hand in one smooth turn). Strike a balance,
roughly **90% meaningful content / 10% fun toys** — the toys enhance the
substance, never bury it.
